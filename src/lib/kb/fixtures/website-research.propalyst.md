## About
Propalyst is a Bangalore-focused premium residential real estate platform that combines an invite-only broker network (RealBroker) with an AI concierge to deliver verified, off‑market listings and end‑to‑end buyer/renter support. The brand positions itself as “broker‑partnered, buyer‑first,” emphasizing privacy, transparency, and curated service for serious home seekers. ([propalyst.com](https://propalyst.com/))

## Products / services
- AI‑enabled concierge that searches the broker network, shields buyer phone numbers, and coordinates agent conversations and private viewings. ([propalyst.com](https://propalyst.com/))
- Verified listing aggregation across partner brokers; weekly verification claimed. ([propalyst.com](https://propalyst.com/))
- Advisory through closing: shortlisting support, price negotiation, and registration assistance. ([propalyst.com](https://propalyst.com/))
- Clear pricing: Buyer fee 1% of transaction value (paid at deed registration); Renter fee one month’s rent (paid at lease sign‑off). These fees are reiterated in the Buyer’s Agreement (MSA). ([propalyst.com](https://propalyst.com/))
- Self‑serve browsing plus concierge model; clients can also view a curated “client report” by phone number. ([propalyst.com](https://propalyst.com/))
