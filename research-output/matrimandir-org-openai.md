## About
Matrimandir is the “Soul of Auroville,” a gold disk–clad spherical structure at the center of Auroville’s Park of Unity, conceived as a place for inner work rather than tourism. The site presents Matrimandir as rising from the earth and “symbolis[ing] the birth of a new consciousness,” with the surrounding Park of Unity, Banyan Tree, Amphitheatre, twelve Petals, and twelve thematic Gardens forming a contemplative landscape. ([matrimandir.org](https://matrimandir.org/))

## Visual cues
- Iconic golden sphere clad with gold disks; inside, an all‑white marble environment suffused with “golden‑pink” light from a translucent inner skin, culminating in a 70 cm crystal globe illuminated by a heliostat beam. ([matrimandir.org](https://matrimandir.org/matrimandir/))
- Surrounds: twelve Petals (virtues such as Sincerity, Humility, Peace) and twelve Gardens (e.g., Progress, Harmony, Wealth, Utility); central Banyan Tree and red‑sandstone Amphitheatre with the Auroville Charter urn. ([matrimandir.org](https://matrimandir.org/the-park-of-unity/))
- The Lake is planned as an isolating ring of water between city bustle and the Park’s tranquility; under phased construction. ([matrimandir.org](https://matrimandir.org/))
- No hex color codes are published; palette is described textually (golden, white marble, golden‑pink light). ([matrimandir.org](https://matrimandir.org/matrimandir/))

## Target audience signals
- Visitors seeking silent, individual concentration (not touristic sightseeing). “It is not a ‘tourist’ site.” Free Viewing Point access via Auroville Visitors’ Centre; electric shuttle available for those who need it. ([matrimandir.org](https://matrimandir.org/how-to-visit/))
- Inner Chamber access requires advance individual booking (open 5 working days ahead; mornings only; no group bookings; children 10+ only). Weather closures apply. Specific recent dates: closed May 18–June 14, 2026 for maintenance; reopened June 15, 2026. Pongal closure January 17, 2026. ([matrimandir.org](https://matrimandir.org/how-to-visit/))

- Donors and volunteers worldwide invited to “join hands” in completing the Park of Unity and Lake. ([matrimandir.org](https://matrimandir.org/how-to-contribute/))
