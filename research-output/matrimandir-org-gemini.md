## About
Matrimandir (meaning "Temple of the Mother") is an edifice of spiritual significance situated at the center of Auroville, an experimental township in Tamil Nadu, India. Conceived in 1965 by Mirra Alfassa (known as "The Mother") and designed with French architect Roger Anger, the structure is a gold-clad sphere rising from the earth, surrounded by a "Park of Unity" consisting of twelve themed gardens and a large artificial lake. 

Referred to as the "soul of Auroville," it serves as a place for individual silent concentration and spiritual discovery. The Matrimandir does not belong to any religion or sect; it is explicitly positioned as a space free from organized religious forms. Built over decades by volunteers and craftsmen, its Inner Chamber houses a 70 cm optically perfect glass globe built by Schott and polished by Zeiss, illuminated by a single ray of sunlight tracked by a computerised heliostat.

## Products / services
Rather than commercial products, Matrimandir provides highly regulated spiritual and observational experiences:
* **Matrimandir Viewing Point:** Free access to a raised garden area offering scenic views of the sphere (requires obtaining a pass after an introductory video).
* **Inner Chamber Concentration:** Access to the inner meditation chamber. Free of cost but strictly regulated (requires 3-5 days advance booking, no group bookings, restricted to ages 10+). 
* **The Park of Unity:** 12 themed gardens (Existence, Consciousness, Bliss, Light, Life, Power, Wealth, Utility, Progress, Youth, Harmony, Perfection), the Garden of the Unexpected (for children), and the Amphitheater. 
* **Quarterly Newsletter:** Updates on the continuing progress of the construction, Heliostat, and gardens.
