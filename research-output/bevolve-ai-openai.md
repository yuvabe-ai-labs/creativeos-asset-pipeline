# https://bevolve.ai/ — OpenAI Research

## About
Bevolve AI is an AI-first sustainability platform and advisory that helps organizations measure emissions and ESG data, generate audit-ready reports across global frameworks, and plan decarbonization—packaged as “Measure. Communicate. Transform.” Their mission is “to democratize impact creation using research and AI,” with solutions spanning ESG reporting, GHG accounting, sustainability/impact communications, PLCA, eco‑social impact services, and a Fractional CSO offering. Presence indicated across EU, Americas, and India; contact numbers listed for the USA and India. ([bevolve.ai](https://bevolve.ai/ai-sustainability-platform/))
