## About
Prakriti Sattva LLC is a holistic wellness and clean beauty brand founded by Supriya Bhat, an Ayurvedic Wellness Practitioner and certified Sri Sri Yoga Instructor with a background in engineering. Rooted in the ancient Indian healing sciences of Ayurveda and Yoga, the brand's mission is to empower individuals to live healthier, happier lives by embracing holistic principles. They aim to bridge the gap between modern life and ancient wisdom by offering personalized wellness consultations, yoga instruction, and handcrafted, chemical-free botanical body and hair care products.
