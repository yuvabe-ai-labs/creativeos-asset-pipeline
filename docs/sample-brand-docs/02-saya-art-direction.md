# Sāya — Visual & Art Direction Guide

> Doc 2 of 3. Feeds `visual_identity`, `target_audience` (esp. `human_casting`),
> and `creative_direction.image` + `creative_direction.video`.

## Visual identity

**Aesthetic:** warm, earthy, artisanal minimalism — apothecary calm, never
sterile.

**Photography style:** editorial macro and tactile lifestyle — real hands, real
texture, shot close.

**Lighting:** natural diffused daylight and soft golden hour. No harsh flash, no
hard studio strobe.

**Visual mood:** serene, tactile, grounded — a slow morning by a window.

**Typography style:** spacious lowercase, large humanist serif for headlines,
generous letter-spacing.

### Colour palette

- **Primary:** turmeric gold `#C8A000`, warm cream `#F5EDD6`, deep clay `#8A4B2F`
- **Secondary:** sage green `#9CA98B`, soft charcoal `#3A3A38`
- **Avoid:** neon / fluorescent anything, cool corporate blue `#0A66C2`,
  pure black `#000000`, clinical white `#FFFFFF`

### Surface & materials

Shoot on and around: **aged linen, pale marble, raw terracotta, unglazed clay,
weathered teak wood, handmade paper.** Tactile, imperfect, hand-touched
surfaces only — no glass, chrome, or acrylic.

### Visual benchmarks

Brands whose *look* we admire (aesthetic peers, not competitors): **Aesop,
Forest Essentials, Kinfolk magazine, Le Labo.**

## Target audience

- **Age range:** 35–55
- **Gender:** women-primary
- **Location:** urban India + South Asian diaspora
- **Lifestyle:** slow-living, wellness-minded professionals who value ritual,
  provenance, and ingredients they can pronounce.
- **Pain points:** dryness and dullness with age, overwhelmed by 10-step
  routines, distrust of over-promising beauty marketing, skin that feels
  neglected.
- **Desires:** a calmer daily ritual, visibly nourished skin, to feel
  represented rather than airbrushed, honest products.

### Human casting (for all brand imagery)

Cast **mature women 35–55, a range of South Asian skin tones, natural styling,
minimal makeup, calm and grounded expression.** Age-appropriate — **realistic
skin texture, visible fine lines, no heavy retouching.** People should look at
ease, mid-ritual, unposed.

## Creative direction — IMAGE

- **Shot style:** macro and process shots; occasional portrait
- **Composition:** flat lay with negative space, or close crop on hands +
  product; rule of thirds
- **Environment:** in-home bathroom shelf and sunlit kitchen; neutral warm void
  for product-only
- **Subjects:** hands applying product, ingredient close-ups (turmeric root,
  crushed herbs), person + product mid-ritual
- **Feel:** tactile, serene, quietly luxurious

## Creative direction — VIDEO

- **Motion style:** slow bloom and subtle organic drift
- **Camera movement:** slow push-in and gentle static holds; occasional
  overhead orbital on a flat lay
- **Transition style:** soft dissolves and match cuts — never hard snappy cuts
- **Atmosphere:** meditative calm, warmth
- **Pacing:** slow and breath-like; let shots linger 3–4 seconds
- **Text system:** INTRO: one-line hook / BODY: one ingredient idea / OUTRO:
  product + gentle CTA
- **Music direction:** soft Indian classical instrumental, 60–70 BPM, sitar and
  tabla, low energy. No Western pop, no EDM, no aggressive percussion.
