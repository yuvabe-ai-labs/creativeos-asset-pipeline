# Sāya — Compliance & Copy Guidelines

> Doc 3 of 3. Feeds `compliance`. This is the most consequential section: every
> `never_use_*` entry is HARD-BLOCKED across all generated assets, so the lists
> are written exhaustively (all inflections).

## Preferred language

**Preferred verbs (default to these):** helps, supports, nourishes, soothes,
softens, comforts, hydrates, replenishes, calms, restores balance.

**Preferred phrases:**
- "supports your skin's natural rhythm"
- "a grounding daily ritual"
- "rooted in Ayurvedic tradition"
- "made for real skin, at every age"

## Never use — WORDS (all inflections hard-blocked)

Cosmetic regulations forbid drug-like claims. Block every inflection of each
root:

- **cure, cures, cured, curing, curative**
- **heal, heals, healed, healing, healer**
- **treat, treats, treated, treating, treatment**
- **anti-aging, antiaging, anti-age**
- **miracle, miraculous**
- **guarantee, guarantees, guaranteed**
- **detox, detoxes, detoxed, detoxifying, detoxification**
- **clinically, clinical**

## Never use — CLAIMS (full patterns)

- "clinically proven to cure"
- "guaranteed results"
- "permanent fix" / "reverses aging permanently"
- "erases wrinkles"
- "doctor recommended"
- "100% effective"

## Never use — TONE

- hype language and exclamation-heavy hard sell
- manufactured urgency ("act now", "last chance", countdown pressure)
- fear-based or body-shaming framing
- pseudo-clinical authority we can't substantiate

## Disclaimers (include where relevant)

- "For external use only."
- "Results may vary from person to person."
- "Patch test before first use."
- "These statements have not been evaluated for the diagnosis or prevention of
  any disease."
