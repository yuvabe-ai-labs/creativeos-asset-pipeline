## Who we are

**Brand name:** Sāya

**Tagline:** *Skin, slowed down.*

**Positioning:** Sāya is a practitioner-led Ayurvedic skincare line for mature
women who want ritual over routine — clean, potent formulas rooted in South
Indian herbal tradition, made for people the beauty industry usually retouches
out of the frame.

**Mission:** We exist to make everyday skincare feel like a grounding daily
ritual, and to represent aging skin honestly instead of erasing it.

**Industry:** beauty (Ayurvedic skincare / wellness)

## Personality

Sāya sounds and feels: **calm, premium, educational, warm, grounded,
practitioner-led.**

(These are adjectives on purpose — no nouns, no phrases. This is the single
source of truth for brand personality.)

## Tone of voice

Warm, knowledgeable, practitioner-led — like a trusted herbalist explaining a
ritual, never clinical or cold, never hype. We teach before we sell. We use
plain language for the science and let the ingredient do the talking.

- We say *why* an ingredient works, then stop.
- We never shout, never manufacture urgency, never promise transformation.
- Lowercase warmth over exclamation points.
