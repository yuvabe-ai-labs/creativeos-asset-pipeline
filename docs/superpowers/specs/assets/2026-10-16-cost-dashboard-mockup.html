What it covers

  • Accounts → clients → canvases, with this month, last month, the change, and how much of each account's credit limit is used.
  • Providers: real bill vs what we recorded, and why they differ.
  • Credit coverage: every feature that costs money (canvas, avatars, scripts, brand kit, research) and whether it charges credits yet.
  • Production and staging, together or one at a time.
  • Any past month, picked from the month menu. Past months never change.
  • CSV download of the accounts table for finance.
  • Refresh now recalculates the current month on the spot. It takes about a minute, only one refresh runs at a time, and the page shows when it last updated and who refreshed it.

Rules to agree on

  • Account, client and canvas figures come from our records. Providers only tell us totals, so they can't be split by client.
  • Rupees use one exchange rate per month, saved with that month.
  • If staging and production share a provider key, its bill is split by how much each one used.
  • AI providers only for now. Hosting (Vercel, Supabase, Trigger.dev) comes later.
  • Refresh now always pulls our own records. Some provider bills only change once a day on the provider's side, so a refresh may not move those.

Build order

  1. Our records. Totals, the accounts and clients table, six-month chart, CSV. Useful from day one.
  2. Provider bills. Billing APIs where they exist (OpenAI, Apify, BytePlus, fal), monthly entry for the rest (Google, Sarvam), balance checks for Kling and ElevenLabs.
  3. Close the gap. Connect the features marked “Not recorded” to the credit system (script copilot, canvas assistant, knowledge base, research, file reading, dubbing voice preview), start charging the ones that only save usage, and count failed jobs the provider still charged for.
