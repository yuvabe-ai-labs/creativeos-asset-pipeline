* Tutorial / How-to → 30–40 seconds

## **5.3 Caption Structure — Recommended Format**

1. Hook line with emoji (1 line)

2. Blank line

3. Body: key benefits or story (3–5 lines, bullet or short paragraph)

4. Brand compliance claims (Handcrafted in USA / No parabens / No animal testing)

5. FDA disclaimer where applicable (product benefit reels)

6. CTA (Shop now / Learn more / Link in bio)

7. Blank line then hashtags (15–20 per post)

## **5.4 Caption SEO & Hashtag Strategy (Instagram 2025/26)**

* ALGORITHM CHANGE: Instagram removed hashtag following in December 2024\. Hashtags no longer appear in follower feeds or drive passive discovery. Use 3–5 tightly relevant hashtags only — quality over quantity.

* Caption SEO is now more powerful than hashtags: embed searchable keywords naturally in the first 1–2 lines (e.g. 'Ayurvedic face moisturiser for dry skin' instead of loading hashtags).

* Branded (always include): \#PrakritiSattva — use in every post

* Instagram Algorithm Note (2025/26): Hashtag following was removed by Instagram in Dec 2024\. Use only 3–5 highly relevant hashtags per post. Keyword-rich captions now drive discovery more than hashtags. Focus on natural language SEO in captions.

* Niche (1–2 per post): pick the most relevant from: \#AyurvedicSkincare \#VataSkin \#PittaSkincare \#AyurvedicHairCare \#NaturalSkincare

* Topic tag (0–1 per post): pick one broad relevant tag only — e.g. \#HolisticBeauty \#CleanBeauty \#NaturalBodyCare \#DrySkincare

* Product or occasion tag (0–1 per post): e.g. \#KeshyaHairOil \#RoseBodyButter \#DiwaliGifts \#MothersDay2027

* Total per post: 3–5 hashtags maximum. Over-tagging no longer improves reach and can appear spammy. Focus on caption keywords instead.

## **5.5 Compliance Rules — Must Follow on Every Reel**

* NEVER use: heals, cures, treats, eliminates (disease/condition)

* ALWAYS use: helps, supports, helps relieve, may help, is known for, is associated with

* For any skin/hair benefit claim, add disclaimer: 'Not evaluated by the FDA. For external use only. Patch test recommended.'

* Safe to highlight without disclaimer: sensory experience, texture, scent, how-to usage, ingredient heritage, Ayurvedic tradition

* Cross-promo opportunity: Periodically direct audience to Ayurvedic Consultations (prakritisattva.com/collections/ayurveda-and-yoga-consultations) — relevant for educational reels about skin types and doshas
